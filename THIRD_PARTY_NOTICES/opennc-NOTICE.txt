Modifications: the shipped file, `opennc-v1-i8.ncw`, is Origon's re-export
of the OpenNC v1 weights into the `.ncw` format
(foundation/media/apm/docs/format.md), with every linear and GRU matrix
quantised to int8 (one symmetric scale per output row); convolutions, biases
and constants keep their f32 values. It runs on Origon's own inference engine
(`apm::denoise`); no upstream code is bundled.

Training data. The upstream weights were trained on the Microsoft DNS
Challenge 4 datasets, MUSAN, and the openSLR 26/28 impulse responses.
Microsoft redistributes the DNS Challenge data under the original terms it
received them. Upstream lists these licences (OpenNC TRAINING.md):

    Read speech      LibriVox                    public domain
    Speech           VCTK                        ODC-By 1.0 (CC BY 4.0 at
                                                 the Edinburgh source)
    Speech           CREMA-D                     ODC Database Contents
                                                 License 1.0
    Noise            AudioSet                    CC BY 4.0
    Noise            Freesound                   CC0
    Music noise      MUSAN                       CC BY 4.0
    Reverb           openSLR 26/28 impulse       Apache 2.0
                     responses

The table above carries upstream's attribution for VCTK, AudioSet, MUSAN and
CREMA-D; upstream's model card is the fuller record. Source: OpenNC NOTICE
